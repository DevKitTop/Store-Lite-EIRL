# BRAND CONTENT PLAYBOOK

**Version:** 1.0.0

This playbook is the single source of truth for Store_Lite brand content. It defines how we
write for our audience, what we publish, and what we never publish. The content-manager skill
grounds every draft on this document before publishing anything.

## Voice / Tone

Store_Lite's voice is:

- **Clear and practical** — concrete steps, not buzzwords. If a sentence could apply to any
  product, cut it.
- **Warm but professional** — friendly without slang or over-familiarity. We respect the owner's
  time and money.
- **Action-oriented** — every piece ends with the next thing the reader can do.
- **Trustworthy** — no hype, no guarantees, no fake urgency. We earn trust by being accurate.
- **Local** — we speak to Peruvian small business realities, not to Silicon Valley.

Tone shifts by format, not by brand: an educational post can be encouraging; a compliance note
must be neutral and factual. The voice itself never changes.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Store_Lite serves **Peruvian small business owners** who:

- Run a storefront, food service, workshop, or service business — often solo or with a small team.
- Need simple, affordable tools to manage their online presence and sell.
- Have little time and limited technical background.
- Want more visibility and sales, but not through complex marketing strategies.
- Value practical guidance in plain language over theory.

They are not agencies, not enterprise marketers, and not power users. Write for the owner who
does the work and the marketing.

## Content Pillars

Every post must map to exactly one primary pillar:

1. **Practical how-tos** — short, concrete tutorials the owner can act on today.
2. **Local business insight** — tips adapted to the Peruvian market context.
3. **Tool and feature highlight** — a Store_Lite capability solving a real, named problem.
4. **Customer success story** — a real example of a Peruvian business using the platform.
5. **Time-saving tips** — efficiency for busy owners who do everything themselves.

If a draft does not clearly belong to one pillar, it is not ready to publish.

## Per-Platform Rules

### TikTok (priority platform)

TikTok is the first platform we publish to. Respect these constraints — do NOT invent
capabilities we do not have:

- **No music selection.** We never recommend, choose, or reference TikTok audio tracks. Content
  is judged on the visuals and the message.
- **Draft-first.** Every piece starts as a draft. We never assume direct publishing is enabled.
- **No native scheduling.** We do not claim TikTok can schedule posts for us.
- **No comment management.** We do not promise comment moderation or auto-replies.
- **Hook in the first three seconds**, then deliver one clear value.
- **Legible text overlays** and clear visuals; assume sound may be off.

### Other platforms

- Adapt the format to the platform; never force a TikTok pattern onto another network.
- Keep the same voice and core message everywhere.
- Use each platform's native conventions without overpromising features.

## Call to Action (CTA) Patterns

- "Try Store_Lite for free" — low barrier, direct.
- "Save time on your storefront today" — leads with the core benefit.
- "Start with a simple draft" — reduces friction and matches the draft-first default.
- "Get your store online in minutes" — specific and outcome-focused.
- "See how Store_Lite works for your business" — invites exploration without pressure.

Use one CTA per post. Never stack several competing asks.

## Off-Brand Examples

| Off-brand                                           | Why it is wrong                              | Correction                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| "10 VIRAL HACKS to explode your sales overnight!!!" | Hype, fake urgency, overpromising            | "3 simple ways to save time on your storefront"          |
| "This secret strategy will make you rich"           | Unverifiable claim, off-brand promise        | "Practical tips to attract more local customers"         |
| "Guaranteed 100K followers"                         | A guarantee we cannot make                   | "Ideas to increase your store's visibility"              |
| Recommending a specific TikTok audio track          | Invented capability — we cannot select music | Focus on the action and value, never on audio            |
| Pushing an instant public post                      | Ignores the draft-first default              | "Start with a draft, review it, then publish when ready" |
| Generic copy that fits any SaaS                     | Violates the ICP and voice                   | Name the Peruvian small business problem explicitly      |

When a draft violates a pillar or a rule above, find the matching row and apply the correction.
