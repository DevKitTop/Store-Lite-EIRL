## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm all critical compliance documents are reviewed.
- [ ] Confirm technical and security controls are implemented or documented.
- [ ] Confirm backup and recovery procedures have been reviewed.
- [ ] Confirm support and incident ownership is documented.
- [ ] Confirm legal and operational review is completed before launch.

---

## Risk

- Legal Risk: Launching without a documented review process may expose the business to avoidable issues.
- Technical Risk: Missing controls may create unstable or insecure production behavior.
- Operational Risk: Poor launch readiness may delay response and increase recovery cost.
