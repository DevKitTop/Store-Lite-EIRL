# Compliance Index

## Overall Project Status

- Status: In Progress
- Target: Safe MVP production readiness for Store Lite
- Approach: Minimum viable compliance controls, legal validation, and operational readiness

## Critical Blockers

- Legal review for Peru has not yet been completed.
- Data handling, retention, and deletion flows are not yet fully documented.
- Security hardening and logging coverage still need formal confirmation.
- Backup, recovery, and incident response ownership remain to be assigned.

## Production Blockers

- Payment and billing disclosure controls require confirmation.
- Consent and account deletion workflows are still pending final documentation.
- Infrastructure controls and environment separation need formal review.

## Pending Decisions

- Who will perform final legal validation for Peru?
- Which retention periods will be adopted for orders, invoices, and customer data?
- Which support and complaint channels will be exposed to customers in production?

## High-Risk Items

- Payment-related compliance gaps
- Weak or incomplete audit trail coverage
- Undefined data deletion and retention policies
- Missing incident response ownership

## Compliance Progress

- Documentation architecture: Completed
- MVP checklist: In Progress
- Security and privacy baseline: In Progress
- Infrastructure controls: In Progress
- Launch readiness review: Pending
